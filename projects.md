  <p class="project-desc">IT and security professionals carry a lot of certifications. Keeping track of renewal dates, continuing education hours, and expiry timelines across multiple credentials is tedious — and the consequences of missing a renewal are real. Skillflow is a self-hosted Django application that centralizes all of it.</p>
  <ul class="project-features">
    <li>Log and track active certifications in one place</li>
    <li>Renewal date monitoring with automatic status alerts</li>
    <li>Continuing education hour tracking per certification</li>
    <li>Admin-managed certification library (vendors + renewal requirements)</li>
    <li>Optional social login via Google, Apple, GitHub, and Reddit</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="project-tech">Python &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Django &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; AGPL-3.0</p>
